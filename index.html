About Us

Rooted in Rajsamand, Trusted Across India

Shree Karni Marbles is based in Rajsamand, Rajasthan — home to one of the largest marble markets in India. We work closely with local quarries to bring you a wide range of marble, granite and natural stone for homes, offices, temples and commercial spaces.

Every slab is selected for its finish, veining and durability, then cut and polished to your exact requirement before it leaves our yard.

  • Wide range of marble, granite & stone tiles
  • Custom cutting, polishing & finishing
  • Packing & transport across India
